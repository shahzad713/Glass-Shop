# Google Business Profile — 12-Week Content / Posting Plan

**Business:** Labbyak Glass & Aluminium
**Goal:** Keep the profile active and trustworthy with genuine updates that help customers decide. Post **1–2 times per week**. Only post real work, real photos, real advice.

## Ground rules (do not break)
- Only post genuine projects and genuine photos taken by Labbyak.
- Before/after posts only when both photos are truly from the same real job.
- No fake urgency ("today only"), no invented prices, no discounts you are not really offering.
- No ranking guarantees, no "#1 in Lahore" claims.
- Get the customer's OK before posting a photo of their property; avoid showing house numbers or anything private.
- Every post ends with a simple call to action: message on WhatsApp **0302 0999713** or request a quotation.

## Post types to rotate
1. Project update (residential) — a real recently completed job.
2. Project update (commercial) — shopfront, office partition, curtain wall.
3. Service highlight — explain one service plainly.
4. Before/after — genuine only.
5. Seasonal maintenance advice — practical tips.
6. Common customer question — answer one FAQ.
7. Quotation CTA post — how to request a quote and what info to send.

## Photo-quality checklist (before any post)
- [ ] Real Labbyak job, not stock.
- [ ] Good daylight, in focus, straight angle.
- [ ] Frame shows the glass/aluminium work clearly.
- [ ] No visible private info (house numbers, faces without consent, documents).
- [ ] Customer permission obtained if it is their property.
- [ ] Landscape orientation where possible; clean, uncluttered background.

## Review-response workflow (run weekly alongside posting)
- [ ] Check for new Google reviews at least twice a week.
- [ ] Respond to every review within a few days — see templates in `REVIEW_WORKFLOW.md`.
- [ ] Thank positive reviewers; address concerns calmly and offer to resolve offline for negative ones.
- [ ] Never argue publicly or reveal private customer details.

## Category / services review (do in Week 1 and Week 12)
- [ ] Confirm primary/secondary categories still correct (see `GBP_ACTION_PLAN.md`).
- [ ] Confirm Services list reflects what you actually sell now.
- [ ] Remove any service you have stopped offering.
