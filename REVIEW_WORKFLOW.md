# Review Acquisition & Response Workflow

**Business:** Labbyak Glass & Aluminium
**Goal:** Earn more genuine Google reviews and respond to every one professionally. Everything here must stay honest and within Google's rules.

## Golden rule
Ask happy customers to share their **honest** experience. Never buy, trade, incentivise, or fake reviews. Genuine reviews build the real reputation that already sits at **4.8 from 27 reviews**.

---

## When to ask
- After a job is finished and the customer is visibly satisfied.
- After a friendly follow-up call/message confirming everything works well.
- Ask once, politely. If they do not respond, do not chase repeatedly.

---

## Review-request message (generic, spoken or written)

> "Thank you for choosing Labbyak Glass & Aluminium. If you were happy with the work, a short honest review on Google would really help other customers find us. It only takes a minute — no pressure at all."

---

## WhatsApp review-request template

> "Hello [Customer name], thank you for choosing Labbyak Glass & Aluminium. We hope you're happy with your [service, e.g. aluminium windows]. If you have a moment, we'd be grateful for an honest review of your experience on our Google profile: [GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK]
>
> Thank you for your support!
> Labbyak Glass & Aluminium — 0302 0999713"

- Replace `[GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK]` with your real Google review short link (owner to generate from the Google Business Profile "Ask for reviews" option and paste here).
- Personalise the name and service each time; do not send bulk identical blasts.

---

## Review response templates

Respond to **every** review. Keep replies short, warm, specific where possible, and free of private customer details.

### Positive review
> "Thank you so much, [Name]! We're glad you're happy with your [service]. It was a pleasure working with you — enjoy the results, and reach us any time at 0302 0999713."

### Neutral review
> "Thank you for your feedback, [Name]. We appreciate you taking the time. If there's anything we could have done better, we'd love to hear from you at 0302 0999713 so we can improve."

### Negative review
> "Thank you for letting us know, [Name], and we're sorry the experience didn't meet your expectations. We'd like to understand what happened and make it right — please contact us directly at 0302 0999713 so we can help."

- Stay calm and polite regardless of tone.
- Do not blame the customer, argue, or reveal order details, address, or other private information publicly.

---

## Negative-review response process
1. **Pause** — do not reply emotionally. Read it fully.
2. **Verify** — check internally whether the customer and job are genuine and what actually happened.
3. **Respond publicly** — short, polite, apologise for the poor experience, invite them to continue offline (phone number above). Post within a few days.
4. **Resolve privately** — call/WhatsApp the customer, understand the issue, offer a fair resolution.
5. **Follow up** — if genuinely resolved, the customer may choose to update their review themselves. **Never pressure or pay them** to change it.

---

## Escalation process
- **Suspected fake / spam / not a real customer:** respond briefly and professionally, then use Google's "Flag as inappropriate" / report option in the Business Profile. Do not accuse publicly.
- **Repeated or serious issue:** the owner handles it personally and decides on any genuine goodwill resolution.
- **Legal or safety concern raised in a review:** owner reviews before any public response.

---

## Prohibited practices (never do these)
- Do **not** buy reviews or pay any service for reviews.
- Do **not** offer discounts, gifts, or any incentive in exchange for a review.
- Do **not** post reviews from employees, family, friends, or the business itself.
- Do **not** write fake reviews or ask others to.
- Do **not** use identical, copy-pasted, keyword-stuffed wording across responses or requests.
- Do **not** invent testimonials on the website or anywhere else.
- Do **not** expose private customer information (name details, address, order specifics) in public replies.
- Do **not** set up review kiosks/stations that filter out unhappy customers ("review gating").

---

- [ ] Check for new reviews (at least twice a week).
- [ ] Reply to each using the right template, personalised.
- [ ] Flag anything clearly fake.
- [ ] Send a genuine review request to any recent satisfied customer (personalised, once).
