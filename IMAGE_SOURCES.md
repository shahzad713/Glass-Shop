# Image Sources & Licensing

Record of every image asset shipped in `/public`. All assets are **self-hosted**
(downloaded into `/public`) — no external hotlinks, no Google Images, no
competitor or watermarked photos. Per `CLAUDE.md`, stock imagery is never
presented as a completed Labbyak project.

Legend:
- **Real** — genuine Labbyak project / product / team photo supplied by the owner.
- **Illustrative** — a genuine local asset reused as a nearest-match thumbnail
  for a service that has no dedicated photo yet. Not passed off as a specific
  completed job; represents the service category generically.

> Replace any `Illustrative` entry with a genuine photo when the owner provides
> one, and flip its row to `Real`.
